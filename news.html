15 May 2026

Annual Sports Day 2026 - A Grand Success

By Admin Sports

The Annual Sports Day at Shree Future Vision Secondary School was held on May 15, 2026, with great enthusiasm and participation from students across all grades. The event showcased the athletic talents of our students...

The event began with a march past led by the school band, followed by various track and field events. Students competed in 100m, 200m, and 400m races, long jump, high jump, and relay races.

The highlight of the day was the inter-house championship, where Red House emerged as the overall winner. Parents and teachers actively participated in special events organized for them.

Principal Dr. Ram Bahadur Thapa congratulated all participants and emphasized the importance of sports in overall development. The event concluded with a prize distribution ceremony.

28 Apr 2026

Students Win Inter-School Science Exhibition

By Admin Academics

Our talented students secured first place in the Inter-School Science Exhibition held at the National Academy Hall on April 28, 2026. The winning project focused on renewable energy solutions...

The team, comprising students from Grade 9 and 10, presented an innovative solar-powered water purification system that impressed all judges. The project demonstrated practical application of scientific principles to solve real-world problems.

Science teacher Mr. Bikash Shrestha guided the students throughout their preparation. The school provided all necessary resources and laboratory support for the project development.

This achievement adds to our school's growing list of academic accomplishments and reinforces our commitment to nurturing scientific temperament among students.

10 Apr 2026

Grade 10 Graduation Ceremony 2026

By Admin Events

A memorable graduation ceremony was held on April 10, 2026, to celebrate the achievements of our Grade 10 students as they prepare for their SEE examinations...

The ceremony was attended by proud parents, teachers, and distinguished guests. Chief Guest Mr. Hari Prasad Sharma, a renowned educationist, delivered an inspiring speech encouraging students to pursue their dreams with dedication.

Merit certificates were awarded to top-performing students in academics, sports, and extracurricular activities. The students shared their experiences and memories of their time at SFV School.

The event concluded with a cultural program featuring performances by junior students, a farewell dinner, and lots of emotional moments as students bid goodbye to their school.
