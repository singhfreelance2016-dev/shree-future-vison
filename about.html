About Us

Discover our story, mission, and the people behind our success

Our History

Founded in 1998, Shree Future Vision Secondary School began with a vision to provide world-class education in the heart of Kathmandu. Starting with just 50 students and 5 teachers, we have grown into one of the most respected educational institutions in the valley.

Over the past 25+ years, we have consistently delivered academic excellence while nurturing creativity, critical thinking, and character development in our students.

Our state-of-the-art campus in Gyaneshwor provides an ideal learning environment with modern facilities that support holistic education.

Our Mission

To provide quality education that empowers students with knowledge, skills, and values necessary to become responsible global citizens and lifelong learners.

Our Vision

To be a leading educational institution in Nepal that sets the standard for academic excellence and holistic development, producing leaders of tomorrow.
